Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions before getting started? Here are answers to common questions about hosting, updates, and timelines.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

Yes. Monthly hosting keeps your site online and secure — websites can get hacked or go down, and someone needs to maintain and update it. Without coding knowledge, you won't be able to make changes yourself, so the monthly plan covers updates and support.

Can I update the website myself after it's built?

Not unless you're willing to learn to code. That's exactly what the monthly care plan is for — I handle updates, fixes, and changes for you.

How long does it take to build a website?

Anywhere from one week to a month, depending on the size of the project and how quickly you provide the content and information I need from you.
